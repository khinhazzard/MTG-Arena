Strategy:
- Play defensively early game
- Use early removals to stop early threats.
- lockdown board or end game with SEA creatures 
- making enemy think I have a Counterspell threat when I have big mana available multiple cards

Waterlogged Teachings Combo:
+ Pact of Negation / Flare of Denial(Sacrifice Blue) (4 mana) -> Enemy plays game-winning spell while you are tapped out
+ Entomb (5 mana) -> Need a big Sea Monster in your graveyard immediately
+ Cling to Dust (5 mana) -> Stop an enemy graveyard reanimation loop or gain emergency life against aggro
+ Sea-Dasher Octopus (6 mana) -> Mutate onto an unblocked attacker to draw a card
+ Dig Through Time (6 mana) -> Search top 7 cards for any 2 cards at instant speed (Assumes 2-mana Delve cost)
+ Harrow (7 mana) -> Missed a land drop or missing colors on enemy turn
+ Tear Asunder (8 mana) -> Exile any dangerous nonland Permanent (6 mana if no kicker)
+ Spell Swindle (9 mana) -> Counter a big spell to make a massive pile of Treasures
+ Cyclonic Rift (11 mana) -> Enemy board is too dangerous and threatening lethal
+ Hullbreaker Horror (11 mana) -> Drop an uncounterable, flash win condition

Notable Combos:
💥 Board Wipe Blowouts vs symmetrical resets
+ (4) Culling Ritual + active tokens = Wipes all cheap permanents to generate a massive mana explosion
+ (4) Damnation + (1) Tamiyo's Safekeeping (5 mana total) = Wipes the entire battlefield while keeping your threat alive

🌊 Whelming Wave Loops vs creature-heavy boards
+ Emergence Zone + (3) Nyx Weaver + (4) Whelming Wave (in graveyard) (7 mana setup + UU) = Cast a surprise, flash-speed asymmetric board clear on an opponent's turn

🪦 Nyx Weaver Graveyard Engines vs unexpected lines of defense
+ Unexpected Counterspell Play (3 mana total) = Sacrifice Nyx Weaver to fetch Pact of Negation from your graveyard when completely tapped out with an empty hand
+ Multi-Turn Counter & Clone Loop (9 mana per turn / 11 mana if drawing) -> (3) Nyx Weaver + (3+) Three Steps Ahead = Sacrifice token Weavers to repeatedly loop Three Steps Ahead from your graveyard on enemy turns
+ Graveyard Command Center (5 mana total + spell cost) -> (3) Nyx Weaver + Emergence Zone = Sacrifice Emergence Zone to cast any card pulled by Nyx Weaver at instant speed on an opponent's turn

🌾 Land-Sacrifice Engine vs needing unblockable damage or infinite card draw
+ (6) Lochmere Serpent + (1) Urborg + (8) Stormtide Leviathan = Every land becomes a simultaneous Island-Swamp sacrifice resource
