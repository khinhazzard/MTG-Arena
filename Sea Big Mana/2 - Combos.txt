Strategy:
- Ramp aggressively into Big Mana.
- Survive the early game with efficient interaction.
- Use the graveyard as a toolbox for Sin.
- Overwhelm the board with massive Sea Monsters.
- Protect your finishers with premium counterspells.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Waterlogged Teachings Targets

+ Pact of Negation (4 mana) -> Stop a game-winning spell while tapped out.
+ Flare of Denial (4 mana) -> Free counter by sacrificing a blue creature.
+ Entomb (5 mana) -> Put the best permanent into your graveyard for Sin.
+ Harrow (7 mana) -> Ramp at instant speed.
+ Beast Within (7 mana) -> Destroy any permanent.
+ Assassin's Trophy (5 mana) -> Destroy any permanent at instant speed.
+ Dig Through Time (8-10 mana) -> Find your best two cards.
+ Spell Swindle (9 mana) -> Counter a bomb and create Treasures.
+ Cyclonic Rift (11 mana) -> Reset overwhelming boards.
+ Hullbreaker Horror (11 mana) -> Flash finisher.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Notable Combos

🌱 Big Mana Explosion
+ Virtue of Strength + Hedron Archive = Stronger mana production and artifact ramp.
+ Virtue of Strength + The Great Henge = Big mana plus a large creature/value engine.

🌊 Sea Monster Value
+ Runo Stromkirk + Waker of Waves = Easy transformation.
+ Runo Stromkirk + Any 6+ mana Sea Monster = Free token every combat.
+ Kiora, Sovereign of the Deep + Sea Monsters = Reduce the mana cost of expensive Sea Monsters.
+ Kiora, Behemoth Beckoner + 4+ power creatures = Draw a card when your big creatures enter.

💥 Board Control
+ Whelming Wave = One-sided board wipe.
+ Slinn Voda (Kicked) = Reset non-Sea creature boards.
+ Cyclonic Rift (Overload) = Reset opposing boards.
+ Stormtide Leviathan + Whelming Wave = Clear opposing creatures while your Sea Monsters remain.
+ Icebreaker Kraken = Lock down opposing creatures/artifacts for a turn.

🌾 Land Value
+ Lumra + Harrow = Recover sacrificed lands.
+ Westvale Abbey = Long-game mana sink / alternate win condition.
+ Exploration + extra lands = Accelerate land development.
